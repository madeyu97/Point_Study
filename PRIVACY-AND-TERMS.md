# PRIVACY POLICY

**Point Study**
Last updated: **[DATE]**

## Who we are

Point Study is run by **[YOUR NAME OR TRADING NAME]**, **[YOUR ADDRESS OR "contactable at the email below"]**. If you have a question about your data, email **[YOUR EMAIL]**.

## What we collect

Only what the app needs to work:

**Your email address.** Used to sign you in and to identify your account. We email you a six-digit code; we don't send marketing.

**Your study progress.** Which cards you've seen, how well you recalled them, when they're next due, your streak, experience points and achievements. Also any personal notes or edits you make to points, and which cards you've archived.

**A username, if you choose one.** Only needed if you want to connect with colleagues. Other users can search for you by username. Your email is never shown to them.

**A licence key** when you activate a purchase, along with the email address attached to that purchase.

We do not collect your name, your location, your device identifiers, or anything about your browsing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party scripts watching what you do.

## What other people can see

Nothing, unless you connect with them, and connections need both people to agree.

Once you accept a connection, that person sees a short summary: your username, streak, level, experience points, how many points you've learned, how many achievements you have, and how many reviews you did this week.

They never see your notes, your edits, which cards you find difficult, your email address, or anything else.

Removing a connection stops the sharing immediately in both directions.

## Where it's kept

Your progress is stored on your device and, if you sign in, backed up to a database hosted by **Supabase**, on servers in **[YOUR SUPABASE REGION — check Project Settings]**. Sign-in emails are sent through **Resend**. The app is hosted by **Netlify**. Licence keys are checked with **Gumroad**.

The app also loads its typefaces from **Google Fonts** and its stroke-order library from **jsDelivr**. As with any web font, those requests reveal your IP address to those services. If you would rather they didn't, we can serve both from our own domain instead — ask.

Each of those companies processes data on our behalf under their own terms.

Access is restricted at the database level, so one user cannot read another user's data even if they try.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. Delete your account and everything goes with it — progress, username, connections and licence record — usually straight away and within 30 days at the latest.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold, correct it, delete it, or send you a copy. Email **[YOUR EMAIL]** and we'll deal with it within one month.

You can also export your own progress at any time from Settings → Backup & restore, without asking us for anything.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data and you're in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

## Age

Point Study is sold to adults. You must be **[18]** or older to buy a licence and create an account. We don't knowingly hold data about children; if you believe we have, email us and we'll delete it.

## Cookies

None. The app stores your progress and sign-in token in your browser's local storage so it works offline. Nothing is used to track you.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect.
